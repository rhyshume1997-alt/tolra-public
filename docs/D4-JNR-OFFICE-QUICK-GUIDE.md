# JNR office: keep this one-page sheet by your screen

**Your job: check the details, save once, check the result.**

1. Use your own login. Check the company is **JNR** before changing anything. If it is wrong, stop and call Rhys.
2. Open the customer/supplier and job/document you need. Check the name, job number, address, amount and date against the request or paperwork.
3. Save once. Read the result. Reopen the record to check it saved. A spinning button or lost connection does not mean success; do not create a second copy.
4. For email, check the recipient, subject, message and PDF. Send once. **Saved as draft** means it has not gone. A sent result still needs a mailbox check if a customer says it is missing. If Tolra cannot confirm sending, stop and ask the admin to check; do not send again.
5. In **Approval Queue**, read the whole item before approving. For a normal email draft sent from the approved office mailbox, use **Mark as Sent Manually** only after sending it. For a chase reminder, follow its review/send steps and keep the approved wording. Never approve a message just to remove it from the list.
6. A customer saying “paid” is a note to check. Compare the bank/payment evidence using the agreed process. Do not mark an invoice paid from the email alone.
7. In **Weekly hours**, check the person and date before changing a time. Use **Add missing** only for a missing event and **Correct** for a wrong event. You can correct your own unlocked records. Only an admin can correct another person's hours. Ask the person about unclear times and give the admin the reason.

**Something wrong?** Tell John / office duty lead. Access problems or another company's data: stop and call Rhys. Use the private D3 contact sheet for numbers.

**Tolra down?** Start the numbered paper register. Tell workshop staff when paper starts and who collects it. When Tolra returns, check saved and waiting-device work first. Enter only missing work and have someone else check it. Keep the sheets and any unresolved items.
