## G16-INV — issue an invoice

Open Invoices and the correct draft. Check customer, order/job, lines, quantity, prices, VAT, dates and terms against the agreed work. Check the preview. Choose **Issue Invoice** and read the confirmation; issuing fixes its contents, gives its number and makes it owed even if not emailed. Confirm once, then read back number/figures. Sending is a separate checked step; do not choose Issue & send just for practice.

## G16-MAIL — review/approve emails

Open Approvals and **Full preview**. Check customer, recipient, subject, exact body, invoice/amount and any attachments; check it was not already sent and no payment claim is awaiting review. Wrong text: Edit/Save Draft and review again. Main’s reviewed screen uses **Open in email app**; send once through the approved route, verify it went, then **Mark as Sent Manually**. If the installed approved workflow offers separate approval/send controls, follow its reviewed instructions. A preview or approval is not proof of delivery. Unknown outcome: hold and ask, never repeatedly send.

## G16-DLN-ORDER — delivery note with a sales order

Delivery Notes → **New delivery note** → **From a sales order**. Select the right order/customer and lines; enter only quantities actually despatched and check address/dates. Review and save once. Read back note number, order link and quantities; inspect Print/PDF. Keep genuine signature evidence when received; never mark signed just to tidy a list. Sending goods can create a draft invoice: check before making another.

## G16-DLN-MANUAL — delivery note without a sales order

Choose **Without an order**. Select customer and optional job; add an invoice number only if one really exists. Check ship/tax dates and each description/quantity. Choose **Save Delivery Note**, read it back and check Print/PDF. No invoice is required first. Do not invent an order or link unrelated records.

## G16-CLAIM — record a customer’s payment claim

A customer saying “paid” is a claim, not a verified receipt. Keep their message, invoice number, stated amount/date/reference in the approved private claim log; note who received it and tell the finance lead. Use the installed reviewed claim workflow if available; otherwise keep a numbered external record and follow the agreed chase-hold process. Do not mark paid, write off, change totals or create a payment from the claim alone. Finance checks bank evidence and allocates an actual payment to the correct invoice separately.

## G16-ERROR — recover from a mistake

Stop; note record number, time and what happened. Read back the saved record/history before retrying. Do not repeatedly click Save/Send. A draft may be corrected through its allowed controls; issued invoices require the authorised correction/credit process, not overwriting. An incorrect payment needs the authorised reversal with a reason. Keep originals and links. Unknown sends, signatures or quantities go to the office lead; database/recovery actions go to Rhys/operator using G13.
