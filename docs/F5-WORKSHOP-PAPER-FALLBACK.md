# Workshop paper fallback: Tolra is down

The office duty lead starts one outage record and tells the workshop when paper starts. Safe work may continue only from confirmed job instructions. Nobody issues guessed job numbers, prices or delivery promises.

## Before the outage: office kit

Keep numbered blank sheets, pens, the private D3 contact sheet and current approved job sheets/drawings in the agreed workshop folder. John names an office entry person and a separate checker. Do not rely on printing from Tolra after it has gone down. Store completed sheets securely; they contain staff and customer information.

## While Tolra is down

1. Tell the supervisor and office. Office writes an outage number, start date/time and person in charge. Use local UK time; include the date for a shift crossing midnight.
2. Each person uses their own sheet. Write real times as they happen. Record a break separately from job time. A line through a mistake plus initials preserves the original; do not erase it.
3. Record job number, drawing/revision, operation, quantity finished/rejected, parts used and problems. Do not claim a step is complete unless it really is. Keep any delivery signature and paperwork together.
4. If the screen says work is waiting to send, mark those paper lines **CHECK DEVICE FIRST**. Keep the device/browser data. Never record the same action repeatedly in the app.
5. Give sheets to the office at shift end or handover. The office keeps a sheet register so no sheet vanishes between shifts. Urgent customer/supplier messages go through the usual office phone/mailbox, with time and recipient recorded. An uncertain Tolra send must be checked first.

## When Tolra returns: office owns entry

1. Rhys/operator confirms Tolra is safe for writes; office records recovery time. Staff continue paper until the office gives a clear changeover time. Do not let some people enter old work while others keep writing it.
2. Collect all numbered sheets and waiting-device details. Let the original device finish sending when safe. Check rejected/waiting entries with the user. Do not clear its data to remove the warning.
3. Compare each line with the actual saved Hours/job record and available history. Mark **ALREADY SAVED** with its matching record/reference, or **MISSING**. An unclear match stays **HOLD**; ask the user/operator. Compare event, person, job and real timestamp, not just a daily total.
4. An authorised office/admin user opens **Hours** for that person and the actual date. Use **Add missing** for absent clock/break events; use the job-time add/edit control for absent job time. Use **Correct** for a wrong saved event. Enter original times, including both dates across midnight; never use today's Start/Stop buttons to recreate yesterday.
5. Enter missing work/quantities through the existing job controls only where the role permits. If there is no suitable correction control, leave that line on HOLD for the supervisor/operator. Do not invent an import or SQL repair. Record the paper outage/sheet/line reference in an available note, otherwise in the secure register alongside the saved record reference.
6. Update stock/delivery/customer records only from signed evidence using normal authorised screens. A paper note saying “paid” or a customer email is evidence to check, not permission to mark an invoice paid. Do not resend a document that may already have gone.
7. A different office person checks every sheet against saved records: shift/break times, job times, overlaps, quantities, parts, completed steps and any messages. Totals must match the paper and company break rule; record explanations for differences. Mark each line checked, with name/time. Reconcile every issued sheet, including void sheets.
8. Office announces normal entry resumes from [time]. Keep originals under JNR's existing records policy. Keep unresolved lines and customer follow-ups open with named owners. Do not destroy paper merely because entry is complete.
