# Tolra evidence map for quality records

ID: JNR-I9 | Version: 0.1 draft | Prepared: 4 November 2026

Proposed owner: John; Rhys checks Tolra facts. Approved by/date ____; effective date ____; next review ____.

Tolra supports JNR’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. No company approval, audit or live validation is recorded here.

**Present** means found in source, not proved in JNR’s installed system. **Partly / gap** names what people or another controlled record must supply. This is support for ISO 9001:2015 records, not proof that a clause is met. Clauses are signposts; use the licensed standard for requirements.
