Tolra supports JNR Engineering’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. This draft is not evidence that JNR follows the process or meets ISO 9001:2015.

**Purpose:** Decide whether the quality system still fits and works.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** John / JNR top management. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. Set planned review dates and bring reviews forward after serious changes or failures. John brings the responsible people and asks for facts before the meeting. Use the I6 first-review agenda.
2. Review past action progress, changes affecting JNR, interested-party needs and whether the policy/system still fit. Include customer feedback, objectives, process results, product conformity, failures/actions, measurements, audits and supplier performance.
3. Review resources, competence, machines/gauges, risks/opportunities, action effectiveness and improvement ideas. At the first post-go-live review, examine validation gaps, incidents, paper reconciliation, permissions, traceability and real backup/restore proof.
4. For each input, record evidence, conclusion and missing information. Do not call missing evidence a pass. Decide whether the system is suitable, adequate and effective; record reasons and unresolved limits.
5. Record decisions on improvement, system changes and resources, with owner, due date and measure of success. Identify any work to stop or restrict pending proof. John assigns authority and tells affected staff.
6. Keep dated minutes/attendees and approval. Check action progress at the agreed dates, verify results and carry unfinished actions to the next review. Approval of minutes is not certification or a software release approval.
