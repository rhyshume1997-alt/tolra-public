## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Stop nonconforming work being used, decide what happens to it, and record it.

**Scope:** Workshop, yard, site, subcontract and delivered work. Numbering and forms: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 2. Responsibilities

Quality lead or named person: NEEDS CONFIRMATION. Disposition authority: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- Stop and hold: label and separate suspect parts or material. Record quantity, job, batch and location.
- Check whether other jobs, stock or delivered parts are affected. Tell the customer when required: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Record a numbered nonconformity linked to the job, inspection and affected items.
- Disposition: rework, scrap, return, or concession. Concessions need the authority in NEEDS CONFIRMATION and any customer approval before release.
- Recheck after rework against the requirement. Record the result.

## 4. Records

Nonconformity log; hold records; disposition and authority; customer approvals; recheck results.
