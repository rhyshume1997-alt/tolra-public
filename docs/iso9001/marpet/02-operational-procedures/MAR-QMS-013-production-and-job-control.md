## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Make sure the right work is done to the approved information, with checks at the right points, in the workshop, the yard and on site.

**Scope:** Cutting, forming, fitting, welding, finishing (if any), yard storage, transport and site work. Methods in use: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
