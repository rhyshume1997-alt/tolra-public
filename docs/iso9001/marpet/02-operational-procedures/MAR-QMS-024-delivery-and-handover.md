## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Deliver and install the right work, check it, and hand it over with the evidence the customer needs.

**Scope:** Delivery to yard or site, installation, site checks, handover to customer or principal contractor. Method: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- Before despatch: check the job, quantities, labels, certificates and inspection release (MAR QMS 015).
- Delivery record: what was sent, to whom, when, and who received it.
- Installation: check fixing, alignment and protection against the requirement. Record site conditions that affect quality.
- Customer or principal contractor sign-off on site: is it used? How? Who signs and what is recorded: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Handover pack: certificates, test or inspection records, snagging list, and any operating or maintenance information required: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Damage to customer property or site: how it is recorded and reported: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- After handover: defects reporting and aftercare: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
