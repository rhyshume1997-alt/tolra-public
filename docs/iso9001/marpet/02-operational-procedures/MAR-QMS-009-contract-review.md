## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Agree what Marpet will build or install, and check Marpet can do it, before accepting work.

**Scope:** Every enquiry, tender, order or variation for work in MAR QMS 001 scope. Reviewer: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 2. Responsibilities

Reviewer: NEEDS CONFIRMATION. Approval of acceptance: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- Record the drawings or specification and revision, quantities, materials, finishes, tolerances, programme and delivery dates.
- Confirm site access, working hours, permits, lifting or access needs, customer property and storage arrangements: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Confirm inspection, hold points, traceability, certificate and customer sign-off requirements: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Check capacity, machines, welders or fitters with the right qualifications, gauges, and approved subcontractors. Record the gaps.
- Resolve differences with the customer in writing before work starts. Record the decision and reviewer.
- Changes after acceptance go through MAR QMS 025.

## 4. Records

Contract review record; customer agreement; difference log; reviewer and date.
