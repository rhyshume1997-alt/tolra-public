## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Make sure bought-in material and outside work meets the requirement before it is used.

**Scope:** Purchase orders and subcontract orders for work in MAR QMS 001 scope. Approver: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 2. Responsibilities

Purchasing lead: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- Purchase orders state the specification and revision, quantity, material grade, certificate needs, traceability needs and delivery to yard or site.
- Welding consumables and other controlled items: confirm how they are identified, stored and issued: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- On receipt: check the order, quantity, condition, certificates and labels. Hold anything doubtful away from use.
- Record acceptance or rejection and who checked it before use.

## 4. Records
