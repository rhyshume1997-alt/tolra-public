## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Plan how Marpet keeps quality work going, and keeps records, after a serious disruption.

**Scope:** Workshop power, yard equipment, site access, key people, records and any software Marpet relies on. Plan owner: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- Disruptions to plan for: fire or flood at workshop or yard; loss of power or equipment; loss of IT or records; key person absence; subcontractor failure; supply shortage.
- For each: what keeps running, what is recovered first, and who decides. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Records backup: where, how often, and how a restore is checked: NEEDS CONFIRMATION. A backup alone is not proof of recovery.
