## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Say which Marpet work, sites and activities the quality management system (QMS) covers, and why anything is left out.

**Scope:** Marpet fabrication and site work: workshop, yard, customer sites, installation, inspection, handover and any subcontracted work. Exact sites and activities: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 2. Responsibilities

Scope owner: NEEDS CONFIRMATION. Approval: top management, NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- List each workshop, yard and typical site where Marpet works. Do not record private addresses in this document.
- List the activities in scope (for example fabrication, welding, cutting, forming, finishing, transport, installation, on-site inspection, handover). Confirm each one.
- State any activity excluded from the QMS and the reason. Check that the exclusion does not affect customer requirements.
- Confirm which customer, contract or legal requirements apply to the work.
- Confirm whether any design work is done by Marpet (see MAR QMS 010) and whether subcontractors are used for any in-scope activity (see MAR QMS 011).

## 4. Records

Approved scope statement with date and version; list of sites and activities.
