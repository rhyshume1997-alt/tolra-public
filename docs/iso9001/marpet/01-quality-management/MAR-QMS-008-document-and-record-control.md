## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Make sure people use the current approved document and that records stay readable and traceable.

**Scope:** All QMS documents, forms and records at workshop, yard, site and in the office. Storage locations: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 2. Responsibilities

Document controller: NEEDS CONFIRMATION. Approval: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- Keep a register of controlled documents and forms (see MAR QMS 028).
- Each document shows reference, version, owner, approval, effective date and review date.
- Confirm how paper copies at yard or site are controlled, when they are replaced and how old copies are removed: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Confirm where records are kept, who can read or change them and how they are backed up (see MAR QMS 026 and 027): NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Corrections: dated note of what changed, why and who approved it. Keep the original readable.

## 4. Records
