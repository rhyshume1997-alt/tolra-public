## 1. Purpose and scope

**Purpose:** Control changes to drawings, methods, materials, subcontractors, site arrangements and documents, so no one works to an old version.

**Scope:** Changes that affect quality in MAR QMS 001 scope. Approvers: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 2. Responsibilities

Change owner: NEEDS CONFIRMATION. Approver: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

## 3. Procedure

_Everything under Procedure is a prompt for Marpet to confirm, replace or delete. It is not a statement of how Marpet works. Marpet's use of Tolra is NOT verified._

- Record the change, reason, and affected jobs, parts, sites, documents and people.
- Review effect on work already done, materials, programme, cost and customer agreement.
- Approve before use. Replace old instructions and remove old copies: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Tell everyone affected and record that they were told.
