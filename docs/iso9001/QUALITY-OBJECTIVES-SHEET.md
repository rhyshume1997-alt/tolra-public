Use measures that fit JNR’s work, risks, customer needs and policy. Agree who collects the data, the source and what counts before comparing periods. Tolra hours, orders and inspection records are inputs, not a complete quality dashboard. Explain objectives to the people doing the work; record results, actions and any changed plan. No missed target may be hidden by changing its counting rule after the event.
