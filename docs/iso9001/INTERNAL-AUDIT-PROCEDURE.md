# Internal audit — 9.2

Document ID: JNR-I4-INTERNAL-AUDIT-PROCEDURE | Version: 0.1 draft | Prepared: 4 November 2026

Proposed owner: John (JNR), with Rhys for Tolra technical facts. Owner appointment, approval, effective date and next review: ____ .

Tolra supports JNR Engineering’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. This draft is not evidence that JNR follows the process or meets ISO 9001:2015.

**Purpose:** Check whether JNR follows its system and whether it works.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** Audit programme owner, appointed by John. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. Plan an audit programme covering the quality system and relevant ISO/customer/JNR requirements. Choose timing from risk, changes, past findings and process importance. Set scope, criteria, method and date for each audit.
2. Choose a competent, impartial auditor who does not audit their own work. A small shop can use another trained person or outside help; John records how independence is protected.
3. Use the I6 checklist and actual samples. Follow one order through drawings, materials, production, inspection, delivery and records. Talk to staff and see the parts and gauges; do not rely only on screens.
4. Write objective evidence: requirement, sample/job/date, what was seen and any gap. Distinguish a finding from a suggestion. Give the report promptly to responsible managers and keep the plan, notes and report.
5. For each finding, agree correction, cause review, action owner and due date without undue delay. Use the nonconformance/corrective-action log and check similar work. Do not erase a finding after it is fixed.
6. Verify actions and effectiveness with evidence. Report overdue or ineffective actions to John and management review. Adjust the next audit plan based on what was learned.
