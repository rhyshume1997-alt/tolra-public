# Purchasing and supplier control — 8.4

Document ID: JNR-I4-PURCHASING-SUPPLIER-CONTROL | Version: 0.1 draft | Prepared: 4 November 2026

Proposed owner: John (JNR), with Rhys for Tolra technical facts. Owner appointment, approval, effective date and next review: ____ .

Tolra supports JNR Engineering’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. This draft is not evidence that JNR follows the process or meets ISO 9001:2015.

**Purpose:** Buy the right material and outside work from suitable suppliers.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** Purchasing lead, appointed by John. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. List what each supplier may provide, including subcontract machining, welding, heat treatment, coating and calibration. Set checks based on how a bad supply could affect the finished part.
2. Before use, assess ability using relevant evidence: capability, sample, past results, certificates or visit. Record approver, scope, conditions and next review date. Default Approved in Tolra is not evidence of assessment.
3. Keep an external supplier evaluation/review log and link it to Tolra’s supplier entry. Only an admin changes Tolra Approved/Conditional/Blocked status. Conditional gives a warning; it does not block an order. Check supplier identity: unlisted text must not bypass JNR’s review.
4. Before issuing a purchase order, check specification/revision, quantity, material, traceability, acceptance checks, certificate needs and any required qualifications or customer access. Tell the supplier what evidence must come back.
5. At receipt, identify the order/batch and check quantity, condition and required evidence. Hold suspect items away from usable stock. Record acceptance or rejection and who checked it before use.
6. Review performance at the agreed date and after a serious issue: quality, delivery, certificates and corrective actions. Record retain/restrict/block decisions and tell affected staff. Reassess before lifting a block.
