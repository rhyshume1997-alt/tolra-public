**Purpose:** Make the right part and keep its identity from material to delivery.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** Workshop lead, appointed by John. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. Before starting, check job number, current approved drawing/revision, CNC program/setup revision, quantity, material and acceptance checks. Check operator competence, machine readiness and gauge status. Stop if anything is unclear or missing.
2. Match received material to its certificate and heat/cast/batch. Label stock, cut pieces, work in progress and finished parts as required. Keep identity through splits, remakes and subcontract work. Fill Tolra material cert/heat fields when traceability is required; inspect the actual evidence too.
3. Follow the approved setup and in-process checks. Record work and times against the right job; check queued/offline entries are saved before entering them again. Preserve measurements and any special-process approval/validation required for welding or other work that cannot be fully checked later.
4. Protect customer/supplier drawings, material and tooling. Record and report loss, damage or unsuitable property. Store, handle, pack and transport parts to prevent damage or mixed batches.
5. Review and authorise production changes before use. Record reason, review, approver and affected parts. Replace old instructions and assess work already made. Put suspect output on hold and use the nonconformance procedure.
6. An authorised inspector records criteria, measurements, result and release authority. Recheck rework. Confirm required certificates/labels and quantity before despatch. Tolra permits some admin early releases: do not treat that permission as JNR/customer approval.
