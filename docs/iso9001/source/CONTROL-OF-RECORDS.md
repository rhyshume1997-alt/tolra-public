Tolra supports JNR Engineering’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. This draft is not evidence that JNR follows the process or meets ISO 9001:2015.

**Purpose:** Keep reliable proof of what was done.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** Office records lead, appointed by John. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. Name the record: job/order ID, type, date and person. Keep the drawing revision, inspection result, material certificate, approval or other evidence linked to the right job. Completed forms are records; do not keep them only on one person’s desktop.
2. Use the agreed private storage location and access list. Check files open and are readable. Protect customer drawings, personal details and commercial information. Record external evidence received from customers and suppliers.
3. Correct mistakes with a dated note saying what changed, why and who approved it. Keep the original readable. Tolra history helps but is not proof that every paper or file change is captured.
4. Use a records schedule: type, owner, location, access, retention trigger/period, backup and disposal rule. John agrees customer/legal periods with the accountant or adviser. Do not invent one period for every record; hold records needed for a claim or investigation.
5. Rhys/operator checks backup success and proves restores include database records and drawing/attachment files. Record the archive, checksum, date, isolated restore and comparison results. A backup script alone is not proof of recovery.
6. At the end of the approved retention period, check holds, obtain named disposal approval and record what was removed and when. Never run broad cleanup or erase audit history as part of this procedure.
