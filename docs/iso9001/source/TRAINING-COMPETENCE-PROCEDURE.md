Tolra supports JNR Engineering’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. This draft is not evidence that JNR follows the process or meets ISO 9001:2015.

**Purpose:** Make sure each person can do their assigned work properly.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** John with workshop/office leads. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. List roles and tasks, including CNC setup/program use, fabrication/welding, inspection, purchasing, order review and Tolra entry. Define needed education, skills, experience or qualifications for each task and customer requirement.
2. Assess each person against those needs using evidence: observed work, sample parts, experience, qualification or practical check. Record scope and limits. Attendance at training alone is not proof of competence.
3. Where there is a gap, plan training, supervised practice or other support. Name trainer, learner, task, due date and safe limits while learning. Do not assign unsupervised work outside assessed ability.
4. After the action, check that it worked using an observed task or other suitable assessment. Record result, assessor/date and evidence. Repeat or change the training when needed.
5. Keep an external skills matrix and assessment records with person, task, evidence, authorisation, expiry/recheck and next action. John authorises actual work; an admin then aligns Tolra roles and inspector scope/reason with that decision.
6. Review competence after task, drawing, machine, process or software changes and after mistakes or long gaps. Remove/restrict access when authority ends. Explain the quality policy, objectives, personal duties and consequences of wrong work.
