Tolra supports JNR Engineering’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. This draft is not evidence that JNR follows the process or meets ISO 9001:2015.

**Purpose:** Contain the problem, put it right and stop it happening again.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** John or named quality lead. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. Stop affected use/despatch. Label and separate suspect parts/material; identify quantity, job, batch, location and what requirement failed. Check whether other jobs, stock or delivered parts may be affected. Tell the right people/customer when needed.
2. Open a numbered nonconformance record outside Tolra and link the job/problem/inspection. Record facts, evidence and immediate containment. A Tolra Problem flag does not block Start, Pause or Finish. Clearing it is not closure of corrective action.
3. An authorised person decides rework, scrap, return or concession. Record decision, person/date and any required customer concession approval before release. A reason typed in Tolra is not customer agreement. After rework, check again against the approved requirements and record the result.
4. Deal with consequences, including delivered work and customer contact. Decide whether corrective action is needed using seriousness, cause, repeat risk and similar processes. Record the reason even when no further action is needed.
5. Find the cause with the people doing the work. Check for similar issues elsewhere. Assign action, owner and due date to remove the cause; “be more careful” alone is not a demonstrated fix. Update risks, instructions or training if needed.
6. A named checker reviews later evidence to see if the action worked. Record sample/period, result and date; reopen if it did not work. Close only after containment, disposition, required approvals and effectiveness checks are complete.
