Tolra supports JNR Engineering’s quality work. Software and documents cannot make a company certified. JNR is the company that gets audited. This draft is not evidence that JNR follows the process or meets ISO 9001:2015.

**Purpose:** Agree the right work before making a promise.

**Responsible role (John must appoint):** John or named order reviewer. Deputy ____; approved by/date ____; effective date ____; review date ____.

1. Save the enquiry, drawing/specification and revision, quantities, materials, tolerances, finishes, due dates, delivery and certificate needs. Ask about traceability, inspection, customer property and legal requirements that apply.
2. Check JNR can do the work: capacity, machines, competence, gauges, special processes and approved outside services. Resolve missing or conflicting details with the customer before accepting. If needs arrive verbally, write them down and confirm them.
3. Compare the customer order with the quote. Record each difference and the agreed answer. Keep the reviewer, date, decision and evidence of customer agreement. A quote’s Accepted status alone is not the full review.
4. Create/link the Tolra quote, order and job records where enabled. Check the job number and required drawing revision reach the workshop. Use the approved alternative record when a module is unavailable.
5. For a change, stop affected work if instructions are unclear. Review its effect on parts already made, materials, cost, delivery and outside work. Get agreement, record the new revision and tell every affected person; remove old instructions.
6. Record complaints and feedback, protect supplied property and track agreed actions after delivery. Refer wrong or suspect parts to the nonconformance procedure.
