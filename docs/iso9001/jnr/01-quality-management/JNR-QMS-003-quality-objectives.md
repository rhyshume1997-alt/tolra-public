## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. Choose objectives that fit JNR's work, risks (JNR QMS 007) and policy (JNR QMS 002). Example topics from the source sheet (on-time delivery, repeat defects, traceability completeness, inspection competence) are examples only. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
2. For each objective write: what it measures, the exact counting rule, the source of the data, the baseline and date, the target and deadline, the actions and resources, the person responsible, and how often it is checked.
3. Do not change a counting rule after a result to hide a missed target. If a rule must change, record the old rule, the new rule and the reason.
4. Record actual results and decisions at each check date. NEEDS CONFIRMATION for the check frequency.
5. Report progress at management review (JNR QMS 022).
