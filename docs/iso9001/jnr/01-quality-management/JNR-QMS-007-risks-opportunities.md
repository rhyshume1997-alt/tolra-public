## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. Agree the rating method before scoring. Draft method for John to approve: likelihood (1 unlikely, 2 possible, 3 likely) times impact (1 small, 2 significant, 3 serious). NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
2. Software risks: each row names the risk, its possible effect, the control and the proof still needed. Examples: another company sees JNR's records; staff approve work without authority; invoice or credit totals are wrong; work restarts without a trace; a wrong drawing reaches the floor; an email changes a financial record; the backup cannot restore records and drawings; paper or queued work is lost or entered twice during an outage.
3. Shop risks John must add: a bad or overdue gauge accepts wrong parts; bad material, outside process or mixed batches reach a customer; machine failure; capacity; welding or special-process qualification; customer change; supply disruption. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
4. For each risk: assess it, choose the control, name the owner and due date, and state how the effect will be checked.
5. Do not use a low score to skip a required release check. Record any risk that is accepted, with the reason and who accepted it. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
6. Review the register before go-live, after a serious change or incident, and at management review.
