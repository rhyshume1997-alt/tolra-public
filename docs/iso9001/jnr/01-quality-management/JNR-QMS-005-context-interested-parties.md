**Purpose and scope.** Record what affects JNR's ability to meet its quality aims, and who has needs that the quality system must meet. JNR's internal and external issues, and interested parties (customers, staff, suppliers, subcontractors, regulators, insurers and others JNR identifies). Content: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

**Source drafts adapted.** Codex #335 (clauses 4.1 and 4.2) and #367 (gap M4-01). Climate-related needs are listed as a prompt only.

## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. List internal issues (for example machines, staff skills, workload, cash flow, systems) and external issues (for example customer demand, materials supply, regulation, economic conditions). NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
2. For each interested party list its needs and expectations, and which of them JNR must meet (legal, customer or its own). NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
3. Note whether climate change is relevant to JNR's work or customers. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
4. Use the list when setting risks (JNR QMS 007), objectives (JNR QMS 003) and scope (JNR QMS 001).
5. Review the list at the management review (JNR QMS 022).
