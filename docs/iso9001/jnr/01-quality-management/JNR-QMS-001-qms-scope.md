## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. List the sites and locations covered. NEEDS CONFIRMATION: sites, buildings, and any outside workshop or site work.
2. List the main work types (for example CNC machined parts, fabrication, welding, outside processes). NEEDS CONFIRMATION: JNR's actual list.
3. For each requirement of ISO 9001:2015 that JNR thinks does not apply, write the reason in one line. Do not mark a requirement not applicable without a reason. Check the wording against the licensed standard.
4. List the customer, legal and regulatory requirements that apply to the work. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
5. Check that the scope matches the quality policy (JNR QMS 002) and the process map (JNR QMS 006).
6. Approve the scope, record the date, and say where the approved copy is kept. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
