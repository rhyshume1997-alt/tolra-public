## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. Set the review dates. Bring a review forward after a serious change or failure. Ask each owner to bring their records before the meeting.
2. Review: previous actions; changes inside and outside JNR; customer feedback; objective results; process results and product conformity; non-conformities and corrective actions; monitoring and gauge status; audit results; supplier performance; resources, people and competence; effectiveness of risk actions; and, for the first review, the Tolra cutover and first-use evidence.
3. For each input, record the evidence, the conclusion and any missing information. Missing evidence is not a pass.
4. Decide whether the quality system is suitable, adequate and effective. Record the reasons and any unresolved limits.
5. Record decisions on improvement, change and resources, with an owner, a due date and a way to check success. Identify any work to stop or restrict until proof is available.
6. Keep dated minutes, attendees and approval. Check action progress at the agreed dates. Carry open actions to the next review. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
