## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. Plan an audit programme covering the whole quality system. Choose timing from risk, change, past findings and process importance. For each audit set scope, criteria, method and date. NEEDS CONFIRMATION for dates.
2. Choose a competent and impartial auditor. A small shop may use another trained person or outside help. Record how independence is protected.
3. Audit against actual work. Follow one job through drawings, material, production, inspection, delivery and records. Talk to staff, look at the parts and the gauges. Do not rely on screens alone.
4. Record objective evidence for each finding: requirement, sample, date, what was seen and the gap. Separate findings from suggestions. Send the report to the managers promptly.
5. Agree correction, cause review, owner and due date for each finding, and record them in the corrective action log (JNR QMS 018).
6. Verify actions and effectiveness with evidence. Report overdue or ineffective actions to the management review. Use what was learned to plan the next audit.
7. Checklist prompts (in JNR's words, for each clause group): is scope, context and the process map defined; is the policy approved and understood; are risks and objectives set and checked; are people, machines and conditions suitable; are gauges valid and traceable; is competence assessed; can staff find the right document; is an order reviewed before work; are suppliers evaluated and their receipts checked; is a physical part traceable to its material and program; are releases and concessions recorded with the right authority; are suspect parts followed to an effective action; is management review held. Results: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
