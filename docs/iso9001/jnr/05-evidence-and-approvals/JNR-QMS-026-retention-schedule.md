## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. List each record type (for example jobs, quotes, orders, inspection records, material certificates, delivery notes with signatures, invoices, purchase orders, supplier evaluations, training records, audit reports, management review minutes, backups). For each: owner, location, access, retention start, period and basis. NEEDS CONFIRMATION for every period.
2. Keep a hold on any record needed for a claim, a complaint or an investigation.
3. Check the retention period has ended and no hold applies. Get a named approval. Record what was disposed of, when and by whom. Do not erase audit history as part of routine clean-up.
4. Review the schedule at the management review (JNR QMS 022).
