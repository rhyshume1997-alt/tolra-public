**Purpose and scope.** Keep a list of every measuring and inspection device, its status, calibration or verification and what happens when one fails. Every gauge, micrometer, caliper, height gauge, CMM or other measuring device used for product checks. List: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

**Source drafts adapted.** Codex #342 (gauge register row), #341 (checklist clause 7.1.5) and #335 (clauses 7.1.5.1 and 7.1.5.2). No Codex register exists yet.

## Responsibilities

- Inspection lead: keeps the register. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- John: approves the method and the impact review. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Inspectors: report any damaged or out-of-date gauge at once.

## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. List each device with its ID, type, range, location, owner, calibration or verification method and frequency, last date, next due date, and status (in use, due, failed, out of service). NEEDS CONFIRMATION for the method.
2. Before use, check the device is in calibration and carries its status label.
3. When a device is overdue or fails, stop using it and review the parts it measured since its last good check. Record the review and the decision.
4. Keep certificates with the register. NEEDS CONFIRMATION for where.
5. Review the register at the management review (JNR QMS 022).

## Tolra support and its limits

- Tolra's inspection measurements do not manage gauge identity, certificates, due dates or bad-gauge impact (Codex #342).
- The register is held outside Tolra until JNR decides otherwise. No gauge module is proposed before go-live (Codex #342).

## Records

- Gauge register with calibration or verification records.
- Impact review records for failed or overdue gauges.
