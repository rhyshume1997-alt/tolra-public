**Purpose and scope.** Keep a record of machine, tool, utility and workshop maintenance so that machines are fit for work and failures can be traced. CNC machines, fabrication equipment, lifting gear, compressors and any other equipment that affects product. List and intervals: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

**Source drafts adapted.** No Codex draft. Source prompt: Codex #335 (clause 7.1.3, infrastructure). Machine scheduling in Tolra is not a maintenance register.

## Responsibilities

- Workshop lead or maintenance owner: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Operators: report faults at once.

## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. List each machine and piece of equipment with its ID, location, owner and the maintenance it needs (routine checks, service intervals, safety inspections, calibration where needed). NEEDS CONFIRMATION for the list and intervals.
2. Record each maintenance task: date, person, what was done and the result. Mark a machine out of service when it is unsafe or unfit.
3. Record any fault that affected a job, and check the parts made since the last good state (JNR QMS 017).
4. Review the register at the management review (JNR QMS 022).
