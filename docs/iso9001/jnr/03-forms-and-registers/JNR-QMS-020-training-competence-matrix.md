**Purpose and scope.** Show what each person is competent to do, the evidence for it, and any limits or expiry dates. Everyone who does product work, inspection, setup, purchasing, order review or Tolra entry. Names and tasks: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

**Source drafts adapted.** Codex #339 (training and competence, clause 7.2), #341 (checklist clause 7.2 to 7.4) and #342 (skills matrix row). Inspector facts from lib/inspectors.ts.

## Responsibilities

- John with workshop and office leads: decides competence and authorises work. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Trainer or assessor: records the assessment.

## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. List roles and tasks, including CNC setup and program use, fabrication and welding, inspection, purchasing, order review and Tolra entry. Define the needed skills or qualifications for each task and any customer requirement.
2. Assess each person against those needs using observed work, sample parts, experience, a qualification or a practical check. Attending training is not, by itself, proof of competence.
3. Where there is a gap, plan training or supervised practice. Name the trainer, the task, the due date and the limits while learning.
4. Check after the training that it worked, using an observed task. Record the result, the assessor and the date.
5. Authorise each person for their work (scope, limits, expiry or recheck date). Then align the Tolra role and inspector scope with that decision. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
