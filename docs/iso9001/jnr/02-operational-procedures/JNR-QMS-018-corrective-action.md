**Purpose and scope.** Find the cause of a real problem, remove it, and check that the fix worked. Every nonconformance or complaint that needs corrective action, plus repeat problems found in audits or reviews. Log numbering and rules: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

- Checker: confirms the fix worked from later evidence.

## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. Decide whether corrective action is needed, using how serious the problem was, whether it is a repeat, and the risk it poses.
2. Find the cause with the people who do the work. Check for similar issues in other jobs or processes.
3. Choose an action that removes the cause (for example a changed instruction, a fixture, a gauge check or training). "Be more careful" alone is not an accepted fix.
4. Name the action owner and due date. Record them in the corrective action log outside Tolra, and link the job.
5. After a set period, the checker reviews later jobs or evidence and records the result. Reopen the action if the fix did not work.
6. Close only when containment, disposition, required approvals and the effectiveness check are complete. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
