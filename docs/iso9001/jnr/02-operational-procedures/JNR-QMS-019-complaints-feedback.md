## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. Log each complaint or item of feedback in a numbered record outside Tolra, with the customer, job, date, what was said and who received it.
2. Check the job, part and records. Contain any affected parts (JNR QMS 017).
3. Respond to the customer. Record what was said, when and by whom. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
4. If the complaint shows a real problem, raise a corrective action (JNR QMS 018).
5. Review the complaint log at the management review (JNR QMS 022). Invoices alone are not a measure of customer satisfaction.
