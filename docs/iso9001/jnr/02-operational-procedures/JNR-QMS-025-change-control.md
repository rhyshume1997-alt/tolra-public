**Purpose and scope.** Review and approve changes to products, processes, documents, software setup and suppliers before they are used, and keep the record. Changes to drawings, CNC programs, setups, processes, suppliers, documents, Tolra settings and staff roles that affect quality. Exceptions: NEEDS CONFIRMATION.

**Source drafts adapted.** Codex #339 (production control, clause 8.5.6), #336 (document revision and approval) and #335 (clause 6.3).

## Responsibilities

- Requester: writes the change request with its reason.
- John or delegate: approves. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
- Checker: reviews the effect on jobs in progress and on parts already made.

## Procedure (draft)

Short working steps. Only what Tolra does is described as fact. Everything else is a prompt for JNR.

1. Write a change request: what changes, why, which jobs, drawings or documents are affected, and the risk.
2. Review the effect: parts already made, material, cost, delivery, outside work, and the customer's agreement where needed.
3. Approve or reject the change. Record the approver and the date. NEEDS CONFIRMATION.
4. Issue the new revision. Remove old instructions and shop copies. Tell affected staff, and train them where needed.
5. Record the change, approval and effective date in the document register (JNR QMS 028) and the job record.
