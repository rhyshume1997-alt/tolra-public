# JNR workshop: keep this one-page sheet by your screen

**Your job: pick the right job, record your time, tell the office about problems.**

1. Sign in with your own login. Check it says **JNR**. Wrong company? Stop and tell the office.
2. Open **Weekly hours** (`/time`). At the start of your shift, press **Clock in** once. Check it shows **Clocked in since** with the right time.
3. Open **Job Board**. Check the job number and instructions against the job sheet. If they differ, ask the supervisor before starting.
4. Use **Start** when you begin that job. Use **Pause** when you stop working on it. In Hours, the job-time buttons are **Start** and **Stop**. Clocking in records your shift; starting a job records what you worked on. You need both.
5. In Hours, press **Start break** when you leave for a break and **End break** when you return. Record the real times; the company rule works out paid time.
6. Tell the supervisor about wrong parts, missing drawings, extra work or a problem. Do not guess a new price or promise a delivery date.
7. At the end of the shift, stop your job time and press **Clock out**. Check today's hours and job times. Tell the office about a missing or wrong entry; do not add a second entry to hide the first.

**After each click:** read the message. If work is waiting to send, keep this browser and device. Do not clear its data or click again to make another copy. Tell the office before adding the same time on paper.

**Tolra down?** Write your name, date, job number, start/stop times, breaks and work done on the numbered paper sheet. Tell the office. Keep the sheet. The office checks what was already saved before entering missing work. Do not enter it again yourself.

**Need help?** Workshop supervisor first, then John / office duty lead. Use the filled-in private contact sheet beside this guide. If you cannot confirm safe instructions, stop that job.
