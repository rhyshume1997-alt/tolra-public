## What the current app does

It works out paid time from the exact recorded times and the agreed break rule. It rounds the finished displayed total to the nearest whole minute. A positive half-minute rounds up. It does not first round clock-in, clock-out and each break and then subtract those rounded figures.

| Exact paid time | Hours/minutes shown |
| --- | --- |
| 7 hours 14 minutes 29 seconds | 7:14 |
| 7 hours 14 minutes 30 seconds | 7:15 |
| 7 hours 14 minutes 59 seconds | 7:15 |

Each daily or weekly figure is rounded once from that figure's exact total. Adding the rounded days can differ from rounding the exact week. Do not adjust a real time record to hide that difference. The payroll export expresses rounded time as hours to two decimal places; decimal hours are not clock minutes: **7.25 hours means 7 hours 15 minutes**. The export is time evidence, not a calculation of wages paid.

Paid versus unpaid breaks is a separate choice. With five exact 08:00–16:00 days and 45 minutes of unpaid breaks each day, paid time is 7:15/day and 36:15/week. With breaks paid it is 8:00/day and 40:00/week. Those exact-minute examples do not by themselves prove the seconds-rounding rule.
