# John and accountant: agree which financial records to keep

**Confirm a minimum six-year rule, its start date and any longer period needed. This is not a date for automatic deletion.** Until the accountant agrees the completed rule, keep the records and ask.

For UK limited-company accounting records, the usual starting point is six years from the end of the financial year they concern. VAT records generally have their own six-year requirement. The accountant must confirm what applies to JNR. Do not count from the Tolra upload or opening day. Assets, unfinished transactions, tax enquiries or disputes can mean keeping records longer.

- [ ] Keep invoices, credit notes, receipts, payments, refunds, payment-to-invoice links, bank-check evidence and VAT/tax reports.
- [ ] Keep the quotes, orders and delivery evidence needed to explain those amounts. Keep SliQ source files/reports, the record-transfer comparison, opening amounts and unfinished-work valuation.
- [ ] Keep original references, dates, lines, totals, attached files and the record of changes/approvals. Do not renumber or replace issued documents to tidy up.
- [ ] John/accountant can find and read an old record and its supporting files even after an old system, subscription or login stops.
- [ ] Access is private and limited to named people. Backups have been restored in practice. A backup alone may be hard to search; a spreadsheet alone may omit files/history.
- [ ] Nothing is destroyed during an enquiry, dispute or other instruction to keep it. At six years, an authorised person checks whether disposal is allowed; it does not happen automatically.
