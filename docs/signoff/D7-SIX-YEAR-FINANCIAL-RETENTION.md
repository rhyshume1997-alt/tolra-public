For UK limited-company accounting records, the usual starting point is six years from the end of the financial year they relate to. VAT records generally have their own six-year requirement. Do not count six years from the Tolra upload or cutover date. Ongoing transactions, assets, enquiries, disputes or other requirements may need longer. The accountant's agreed rule below controls; if unsure, keep the record and ask.

- [ ] Keep sales/purchase invoices, credit notes, receipts, payments, refunds, allocations, bank reconciliation evidence and VAT/tax reports.
- [ ] Keep linked quotes/orders/delivery evidence where needed to explain those figures, source SliQ exports/reports, transfer mappings and opening balances/unfinished-work valuation.
- [ ] Keep original references, issue dates, line amounts, totals, attachments and change/approval trail so we can explain what happened. Do not reset numbering or replace an issued document to tidy it up.
- [ ] Accountant and John can retrieve an old record and its supporting evidence in a readable form, even after a login, provider subscription or old system stops.
- [ ] Keep the archive private with named access, protected backups, tested restore and an owner who checks readability. A backup alone is not a searchable complete archive; a spreadsheet alone may lose attachments/history.
- [ ] No destruction while a tax enquiry, dispute or other hold applies. No automatic deletion at six years without a checked disposal decision.
