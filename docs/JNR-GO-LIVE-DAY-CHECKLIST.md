# JNR: first day with Tolra

**Thursday 12 November 2026 — UK time**

Keep this open beside Tolra. Tick one box at a time. Use real work only when
Rhys says the system is ready. If you are practising, use the agreed practice
company. Do not put pretend payments into JNR's live records.

## Before the team starts

- [ ] Rhys has confirmed that the release is ready and the checks have passed.
- [ ] You have the correct Tolra address and your own sign-in details.
- [ ] The office knows who will check payments, send emails and help staff.
- [ ] Fill in the contact card below. Do not guess a phone number.

| If you need help | Person | Phone or agreed way to reach them |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sign-in, setup or a Tolra problem | Rhys / agreed Tolra contact | Fill in before the first shift |
| Job details, drawings or the shop floor | JNR supervisor | Fill in before the first shift |

## 1. First sign-in

- [ ] Open the address Rhys gave you. Enter your username or email and password.
- [ ] Change your password if asked. Set up the extra sign-in check if asked.
- [ ] Choose the correct company and view if Tolra asks.
- [ ] Check the company name says JNR. Stop if it is the wrong company.
- [ ] Sign out and back in once so you know your details work. Keep your password
      to yourself.

If sign-in fails, ask Rhys. Do not use somebody else's account.

## 2. Check the company details

Ask an admin to open **Settings**.

- [ ] Check the business name, address, phone and email.
- [ ] Check the VAT details, payment terms and bank details with accounts.
- [ ] Check the logo and the words printed on quotes and invoices.
- [ ] Save, reopen and check the saved details. Check a document preview too.

Changing a bank number is not a way to test the screen. Use the correct details.

## 3. Add the team

An admin opens **Admin > Users**, then **New user**.

- [ ] Add each person's name and their email or username.
- [ ] Choose what they need: workshop for shop-floor work, office for office
      work, admin only for people who look after the company setup.
- [ ] Save the person. Give them their own sign-in details privately.
- [ ] Ask them to sign in and check they can see the pages they need.

The Add user screen currently says no email is sent. Do not wait for an invite
unless the screen says an invite has actually been sent.

## 4. Add the first customer

- [ ] Open **Customers** and look for them first. Do not add the same customer twice.
- [ ] If they are new, choose **New customer**.
- [ ] Enter their name, address, contact and email. Check the email carefully.
- [ ] Save. Open the customer again and check the details.

## 5. Make the first quote

- [ ] Open **Quotes > New quote**. Choose the right customer.
- [ ] Add a clear description, quantities, prices, VAT and the valid-until date.
- [ ] If using **Price this part** or the price calculator, check its costs and
      quantity before adding the price to the quote.
- [ ] Choose **Save Quote**. Read the saved draft and its preview.
- [ ] Ask the person who agrees prices to check it before it goes to the customer.

Saving a quote makes a draft. It does not send it.

## 6. Make the first job

- [ ] Open **Jobs > New job** or use the agreed quote-to-job route.
- [ ] Check the customer, part, quantity and due date.
- [ ] Link the agreed quote or order if there is one. Add the drawing and job notes.
- [ ] Save. Ask the supervisor to check the job before anyone starts it.
- [ ] The person doing the work checks it is the right job before starting time.

## 7. Make the first invoice

- [ ] Check with accounts that the work is ready to invoice.
- [ ] Open **Invoices > New invoice**, or the agreed job-to-invoice route.
- [ ] Check the customer, work, quantities, prices, VAT, due date and bank details.
- [ ] Save the draft and read its preview. Ask accounts to check it.
- [ ] Issue and send it only when accounts agrees. Do not record money that has
      not arrived.

## 8. Check an email before it goes

Use the chasing controls only when they are available in this release.

- [ ] Open **Approvals**. Check the customer, invoice, address and full message.
- [ ] If needed, choose **Edit**, then **Save draft**. Read the saved words again.
- [ ] **Approve and send** means “I have checked this; send this email now”.
      It does not mean “mark this invoice paid”. Wait for the result.
- [ ] If sending is not set up, the draft stays. Follow the manual-send message;
      mark it sent manually only after sending it in your email app.
- [ ] **Skip** puts a reminder aside. **Pause chasing for this customer** affects
      that customer's chasing, not just the draft on screen.

If a customer says they have paid, choose **Customer says paid** on the invoice.
**Payment to confirm** means chasing waits while accounts checks the bank.
**Confirm payment received** opens the payment form in the wired chase screen:
check the amount and date, then save the payment. Cancelling leaves the claim open.
If no money arrived, choose **Not received, resume chasing**.

## If anything goes wrong

Stop that step. Note the page, invoice or job number, what you clicked and the
message shown. Tell the right person on the contact card. Do not send passwords
or bank statements in a general group chat.

If an email or payment may already have been saved, ask accounts to check before
trying again. This avoids sending twice or recording the same money twice.
