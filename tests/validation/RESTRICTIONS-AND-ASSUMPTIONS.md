## Assumptions

### A1. Money rounding rule (decision by Rhys, 4 Nov 2026)

"Money always rounds to the nearest penny, with halves going up (0.135 becomes 0.14). The app and the database both do this, exactly as they do today. No change."

In technical words: round half away from zero to 2 decimal places, the same in the app (`lib/money.ts`, integer arithmetic) and in the database (Postgres `round(numeric, 2)`). Consequences for this pack:

- URS-003 means "exact pence, half away from zero". It does not mean bankers' (half-to-even) rounding. OQ-003 compares saved and printed totals against this rule.
- The private handover records 38 tie cases generated by running the real `lib/money.ts` that agree with SQL, 21 of which would differ under half-to-even. That reconciliation was not re-run by this pack and is not a result of IQ/OQ.
- Half-to-even appears only in the deferred costing engine (release 143, not in this cutover), which deliberately matches the original engine; its golden tests are untouched. A reconciliation helper that uses half-to-even must not be used for money.
- The rounding source files were checked only by the CI diff audit for the final SHA, which reported no rounding code changed. That is a recorded CI line, not a sign-off.

### A2. Other assumptions

- The test environment is a disposable copy with fake data. No production system, real customer record, real key or secret is used by any step here.
- The pack executes only mapped unit files itself. Database, browser and manual rows are performed on the disposable backend by the named person and recorded in the results file.
- Rhys alone signs the VSR. The process owner (John) performs PQ. No result, decision or signature is pre-filled.
